# Why this exists

Some banks won't take your whole password. Instead they ask for a few
characters from it: "enter the 3rd, 7th and 12th characters". These
are called [masked passwords], and counting along a long password to
find character 12 is fiddly and easy to get wrong.

So type or paste your password here, tap the positions you're asked
for, and read them off.

Use a strong password while you're at it. I'm a fan of "[correct horse
battery staple]" style passphrases (just not that one, it's taken).

Wary of typing a password into a website? Good. Here's [how it works],
and the code is [open source on GitHub].
